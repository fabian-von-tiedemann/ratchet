## Question

The control room proposed releasing 0.16 next week, at the pace of at most one release a week. The maintainer answered in Swedish, in two messages:

> eller släpp när det är redo

> jag vill ju närma mig 1.0 såklart

Translation: "or release when it is ready". "I want to get closer to 1.0, of course."

The control room then put this to the maintainer, in Swedish. It is quoted with the table of the six criteria of D-2026-11-19-one-point-oh left out, as the control room's own elision:

> Ja, vi släpper när det är redo. Ditt ord räcker, eftersom AGENTS.md redan tillåter en release när du ber om det, och jag skriver in det som ett beslut. […]
>
> Två av kriterierna är tid och verklig användning. Dem kan vi bara få igång tidigt, inte skynda på. Resten är arbete här.

> 2. **Kontrollerna för 1.0: #70, #71 och #72.** De är små kontroller som läser taggarna och registret, och varje plan granskas först.
> 3. **Startreglerna som ännu bara är text.** Hemlighetsskanning i varje PR och varje natt (`ratchet.no-secrets`) och en kontroll av beslutsfilernas format (`ratchet.decision-as-file`) är de som går snabbast att bygga.
> 4. **Frys formatet.** Efter 0.16 ändrar vi inte formatet på `ratchet.json` och `register.json` om det inte är nödvändigt. Då börjar räkningen av tre taggar och 30 dagar.

Translation: Yes, we release when it is ready. Your word is enough, since AGENTS.md already allows a release when you ask for one, and I will write it down as a decision. […] Two of the criteria are time and real use. We can only start those early, not hurry them. The rest is work here. My proposed order:
1. The map-only mode (in progress). It makes it easy for your colleague, or your next project, to start using Ratchet. The two criteria of real use come from there, so it is best to start now.
2. The checks for 1.0: #70, #71 and #72. They are small checks that read the tags and the register, and each plan is reviewed first.
3. The start rules that are still text only. A secret scan in every PR and every night (`ratchet.no-secrets`) and a check of the decision files' format (`ratchet.decision-as-file`) are the quickest to build.
4. Freeze the form. After 0.16 we do not change the form of `ratchet.json` and `register.json` unless it is necessary. Then the count of three tags and 30 days begins.
5. Release 0.16 when the map-only mode is in.

Answer "yes to your advice", and I will write the decision on releasing when ready, and the plan toward 1.0, into a decision file. […]

## Answer

> ja på råd

Translation: yes, on the advice.

## What follows

- **Releases.** The control room proposes a release when a batch is ready, without waiting for a week to pass. This is the maintainer asking, which AGENTS.md "How to release a version" already allows. Each release is still a pull request of its own, merged only on the maintainer's word.
- **The order of the work toward 1.0** is the five points above, in that order. The criteria themselves stand as D-2026-11-19-one-point-oh set them.
- **The form.** From 0.16 on, the form of `ratchet.json` and `register.json` is changed only when a change is necessary. Each such change needs the maintainer's word on that change, and restarts the count of the first criterion. A new data file of its own, such as the map of the map-only mode, is not a change of the form of either file.
- **Each check built toward 1.0** goes through a review of its plan before it is built (D-2026-11-25-plan-review-before-build).
