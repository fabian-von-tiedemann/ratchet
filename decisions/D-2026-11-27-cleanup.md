# D-2026-11-27-cleanup: read the register once, let the tests be the list of cases, and generate the register fixtures

- Date: 2026-11-27
- Decided by: Ratchet's maintainer
- Kind: decision (D)

## Question

The maintainer wrote, in Swedish, that the repo feels like it only grows, and asked for a proper clean-out of what is needed and what is not. Two assessments in fresh contexts, by models from two families, inventoried the repo. Their material belongs to the control room and is not part of this repo. The control room then put this to the maintainer, in Swedish. Its summary of the two assessments before this is left out:

> **Mina råd**
> 1. **Läs registret en gång och korta AGENTS.md.** Läslistan slutar be agenten läsa REGISTER.md separat, eftersom blocket redan finns i AGENTS.md. Avsnittet "The main branch" krymps till pekare. Texterna i registrets `where` kortas, till exempel de sju rader som börjar med samma 49 ord. *Mitt råd: ja, först.* Det kräver inget beslut och sparar ungefär 6 000 ord i varje session.
> 2. **Låt testerna vara listan över fall.** `checks/README.md` behåller löftet, hotet och gränserna för varje kontroll, och tabellerna med fall ersätts av länkar till testerna. Underhållsdelarna flyttar till `maintain/README.md`. *Mitt råd: ja.* Det är samma princip som i beslutet om inaktuell text: varje fakta står på ett ställe. Det sparar 10 000–30 000 ord och kräver ett beslut om var den slutliga listan över fall finns.
> 3. **Skapa testregistren när testerna körs.** *Mitt råd: ja, men efter 1 och 2,* och med en plan som visar att `base` fortfarande bedömer rätt. Det ändrar texten i `repo.rules-as-data`, så det kräver ditt beslut.
> 4. **Sidtesterna (2 462 rader) och bevakningen av källor.** *Mitt råd: vänta.* Landningssidan skulle vänta enligt ett tidigare beslut, och bevakningen är en tjänst som fungerar.
> 5. **Det som redan har ett datum.** Rensningen av experimentella regler öppnar den 15 januari enligt `repo.subtract`, och `form_age.py` tas bort efter 1.0. *Mitt råd: låt det ske som planerat.*
>
> Svara till exempel **"ja på dina råd"**. Då skriver jag beslutet för punkt 2 och 3, och planen för punkt 1 granskas innan den byggs.

Translation:
1. **Read the register once, and shorten AGENTS.md.** The read-first list stops asking the agent to read REGISTER.md separately, since the block already stands in AGENTS.md. The section "The main branch" shrinks to pointers. The register's `where` texts are shortened, for example the seven rows that open with the same 49 words. *My advice: yes, first.* It needs no decision and saves about 6,000 words in every session.
2. **Let the tests be the list of cases.** `checks/README.md` keeps each check's claim, threat and limits, and its tables of cases are replaced by links to the tests. The maintenance parts move to `maintain/README.md`. *My advice: yes.* It is the principle of the decision on stale text: each fact stands in one place. It saves 10,000 to 30,000 words, and needs a decision on where the finite list of cases lives.
3. **Generate the register fixtures when the tests run.** *My advice: yes, but after 1 and 2,* and with a plan that shows that `base` still judges correctly. It changes the text of `repo.rules-as-data`, so it needs your decision.
4. **The page tests (2,462 lines) and the watch of sources.** *My advice: wait.* The landing page was to wait under an earlier decision, and the watch is a service that works.
5. **What already has a date.** The clean-out of experimental rules opens on 15 January under `repo.subtract`, and `form_age.py` is removed after 1.0. *My advice: let it happen as planned.*

Answer, for example, "yes to your advice". I will then write the decision for points 2 and 3, and the plan for point 1 is reviewed before it is built.

## Answer

> japå dina råd tack

Translation: yes to your advice, thanks.

## What follows

- **Point 1**, then **point 2**, then **point 3**, each a pull request of its own with a reviewed plan.
- **Point 2.** The finite list of cases of a check lives in its tests. `checks/README.md` states each check's claim, threat and limits, and links to the tests that hold its cases. Where a test reads a table in `checks/README.md` today, the plan says where that list moves.
- **Point 3.** The register fixtures under `checks/fixtures/register/` are made when the tests run, not tracked. `repo.rules-as-data` is reworded to match, and the plan must show that `base` still judges a pull request by the base branch's fixtures.
- **Points 4 and 5** wait, as advised.
